A walk without a conclusion

May 29 · Lumen demo journal

An unhurried circuit of the neighbourhood and three details I had passed a hundred times.

I opened the notebook before breakfast, while the light still fell across the table at an angle. After a few minutes I had a list of small things I meant to forget. It turned out those were the ones worth keeping.

Something to return to

The best notes leave room to come back. They hold enough detail to bring a moment into focus and enough open space for an idea you do not have yet. The next time you notice something familiar, try naming one detail that changed.

A page can hold an observation without demanding an explanation.

The point is to make a record that you will want to read again. One sentence can do that work.
