A quieter corner of the ecosystem

Built for people.
Open to ideas.

Epoch is an independent publication theme for Solana communities, curious readers and the people building what comes next.

Make it yours.

This is a sample About page. Introduce your editors, explain your editorial process and tell readers how to get in touch. Replace the demo content with your own work before publishing.

A publication, not a trading interface.

The theme has no wallet connection, price feed or trading integration. Add links to your community and publish useful stories at your own pace.

Open by design.

Epoch is free software under GPL-2.0-or-later. Its source, artwork and installation guides are included. The footer credit can be removed without breaking your site.
