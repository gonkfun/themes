About

A notebook,
kept in public.

Fieldnote is a free theme for naturalists, gardeners, walkers and anyone who likes to write down what they notice outdoors.

Made for observation.

Entries are specimen cards, with room for the date, place and weather of each observation. Articles support margin notes for the asides that always come up in the field.

Quiet by design.

No tracking, no external fonts and no ads. The day/dusk switch and the season label run entirely in the reader’s browser.

Yours to change.

Fieldnote is free software under GPL-2.0-or-later. Edit the colours, the artwork, the wording or the footer credit.
